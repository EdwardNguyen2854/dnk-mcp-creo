**Research date:** 2026-10-04. **Scope:** only the candidates named in [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md#L33-L41). This is a catalog-status note, not a standards-profile selection or a conformity assessment.

ISO's [stage-code reference](https://www.iso.org/stage-codes.html) labels **90.92** as an International Standard to be revised and **90.93** as confirmed. ISO's published lifecycle on the 128-1 page explicitly says that its confirmed version remains current. A revision or draft stage is not itself a published successor or evidence that the listed edition has been withdrawn.

## Inaccessible or uncertain catalog data

- ISO's detail RSS feeds were accessible and expose the exact publication reference/title and latest stage event. Most full catalog HTML pages and several OBP views returned HTTP 403 to this research session. The ISO 128-1 catalog page and the ISO 8015 and ISO 5456-2 OBP views were readable.
- Except where stated (ISO 8015:2011 second edition; ISO 128-1:2020 Edition 2), the accessible feed does not expose the ordinal edition number or full publication date/month. The table therefore records the exact year-designated publication reference and does not guess edition ordinals.
- ISO 128 is a multipart family. The entries for parts 1 and 3 are catalog examples, not a complete inventory or a recommendation of which part(s) apply.
- For ISO 2768, the yearless record/feed does not establish in the accessible data whether the project at stage 60.00 is the final successor publication. No successor year/designation is asserted.
- ISO/DIS 16792 and ISO/CD 21920-1/-2/-3 are draft records, not published successor editions. ISO 129-1's 90.92 event indicates revision, but no year-designated replacement publication was established in this review.

## Decisions still requiring the design authority

As the roadmap states, catalog status does **not** settle which standards or parts apply, which editions the project adopts, or what customer/company overlays apply. The design authority must determine applicability, adopted edition(s), any overlays, and approval authority before profile sign-off. This note does not choose tolerance, projection, units, sheet, title-block, datum, or surface-texture defaults; it does not infer customer/company adoption or declare conformity.
